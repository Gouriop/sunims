# Suni Jose &bull; Dark Editorial Bio-Link Web App

A bold, dark-themed personal link and profile hub for **Suni Jose**, Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Business Systems (CSBS) at Christ College of Engineering.

---

### 🌟 Design & Theme
- **Theme:** Peacock Green (`#00b49f`) and Crisp White (`#ffffff`) on Deep Matte Dark Slate (`#070e12`).
- **Typography:** Google Fonts *Bebas Neue* for high-impact condensed hero names (`SUNI` in white, `JOSE` in peacock green) and *Plus Jakarta Sans* for readable, modern body copy.
- **Hero Photo:** `sunimiss.png` portrait with smooth multi-directional gradient vignette fading naturally into the dark background.
- **Academic & Social Links:** Direct links to LinkedIn, Google Scholar, and Christ College of Engineering.

---

### 🔘 Action Buttons
- **Peacock Green Primary Button:** Christ College of Engineering (`https://cce.edu.in`). Custom buttons can be added anytime in Editor Mode.

---

### 🔒 Editor Login & Real-Time Cloud Sync
- Click **`Login`** in the top-right corner.
- **Passcode:** `sunikkutty123`
- **Real-Time Cross-Device Sync:**
  - Whenever you add a link, remove a link, update info, or upload a photo, it is automatically synced to the cloud in real-time.
  - Anyone who opens or clicks your link from WhatsApp, LinkedIn, mobile, or desktop will see the latest updates instantly!
- **When Unlocked (Editor Mode):**
  - **`Add Link`**: Add custom buttons (choose Primary Peacock Green or Outline).
  - **`Delete Link`**: Hover over any button and click `×` to remove.
  - **`Change Photo`**: Click the camera pill on the right side to upload Suni Jose's picture from your computer or phone.
  - **`Edit Info`**: Update name, title, department, bio, phone, email, or Google Maps location URL.
